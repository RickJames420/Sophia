About Bloom Valley Nursery

For over two decades, Bloom Valley Nursery has been the trusted source for quality plants, trees, and gardening supplies in our community. Our family owned business is dedicated to helping both new and experienced gardeners create beautiful gardens.

Our Story

Founded by plant enthusiasts Sarah and Michael Bloom, our nursery began as a small backyard operation. Today, we've grown into a large garden center serving thousands of satisfied customers throughout the region.

Our Mission

To provide the highest quality plants and gardening supplies while sharing our knowledge and passion for horticulture with our community. We believe that everyone deserves access to beautiful, healthy plants that bring joy and life to their homes and gardens.

What Sets Us Apart

  • Expert horticultural staff with decades of combined experience
  • Locally grown plants adapted to our climate
  • Comprehensive plant care guidance and support
  • Custom landscaping consultation services
  • Community-focused approach to business
